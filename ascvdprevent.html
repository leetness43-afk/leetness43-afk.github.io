PREVENT™ vs. Pooled Cohort Equations

The 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline replaces the 2013 PCE with the race-free AHA PREVENT™ equations (ages 30–79, incorporating eGFR and statin use). Because PREVENT is better calibrated and yields lower percentages than PCE, treatment cutoffs shifted from 5% / 7.5% / 20% (PCE) to 3% / 5% / 10% (PREVENT-ASCVD).

Reference: 2026 ACC/AHA Primary Prevention Risk Tiers & Treatment Thresholds

Comparison of 2026 PREVENT-ASCVD cutoffs vs. legacy 2013 PCE cutoffs, lipid goals, CAC role, and blood pressure thresholds.

Risk Category 2026 PREVENT-ASCVD Legacy 2013 PCE Lipid-Lowering Recommendation LDL-C & Non-HDL-C Goals Role of CAC & Other Preventive Care
Low Risk < 3% < 5% Health-behavior counseling (Class 1). Consider moderate-intensity statin if age 30–59 with persistent LDL-C 160–189 mg/dL or 30-yr risk ≥10%. If treated: LDL-C <100 mg/dL; Non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL. CAC generally not needed unless strong family Hx of premature ASCVD or elevated Lp(a).
Borderline Risk 3% to < 5% 5% to < 7.5% Moderate-intensity statin reasonable (Class 2a/2b) after clinician–patient discussion, especially if risk enhancers are present. LDL-C <100 mg/dL (≥30% drop); Non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL. Order CAC if decision is uncertain (M ≥40, F ≥45). CAC = 0 allows deferral; CAC ≥1 supports statin.
Intermediate Risk 5% to < 10% 7.5% to < 20% Moderate- to high-intensity statin recommended (Class 1) after clinician–patient risk discussion. LDL-C <100 mg/dL (≥30% drop); Non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL (aim <70 if CAC ≥100). Order CAC if intensity/initiation uncertain. Start BP meds if Stage 1 HTN (≥130/80) and PREVENT Total CVD ≥7.5%.
High Risk ≥ 10% ≥ 20% High-intensity statin recommended (Class 1). Add ezetimibe / PCSK9 mAb / bempedoic acid if goals unmet. LDL-C <70 mg/dL (≥50% drop); Non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL; ApoB <70 if TG 150–499. CAC not needed (treat directly). Treat BP to <130/80 mmHg. Consider low-dose aspirin (81 mg) only if age 40–70 with low bleeding risk.
Direct-Treatment Conditions (No Score Needed) Bypass Risk Calculator 1) Clinical ASCVD: High-intensity statin; goal LDL-C <70 mg/dL (or <55 mg/dL if very high risk).
2) LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL / FH: High-intensity statin; goal LDL-C <100 mg/dL (<70 if risk factors/CAC).
3) Diabetes, CKD Stage 3–4, or HIV (age 40–75): At least moderate-intensity statin; intensify to high-intensity (goal <70) if multiple risk factors or PREVENT ≥10%.

Sources: Khan SS et al., Circulation 2024;149:430–449 (AHA PREVENT™ Base Equations); 2026 ACC/AHA Multisociety Guideline on the Management of Dyslipidemia; 2025 AHA/ACC High Blood Pressure Guideline; Goff DC et al., Circulation 2014 (2013 Pooled Cohort Equations). For clinician decision support only.
