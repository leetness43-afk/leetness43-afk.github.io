Physiologic range is typically 10–12 mg/m²/day.

~Two-thirds of the total is given on waking.

Hydrocortisone Replacement Plan

Sick-day rules: Double the oral dose for fever >38°C and triple for >39°C or significant illness. Patients should carry injectable hydrocortisone for adrenal crisis.
Note: Maintenance dosing is titrated to clinical well-being—serum cortisol and ACTH do not guide chronic replacement. Primary adrenal insufficiency additionally requires mineralocorticoid replacement (fludrocortisone). Congenital adrenal hyperplasia often needs higher doses; secondary insufficiency often needs lower doses.
