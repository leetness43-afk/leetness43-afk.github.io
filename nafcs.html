Reference 1: Official NAFCS Scoring Rubric (Hegele et al., 2024)

Maximum score = 100 points. Infants (<1 year) follow a separate clinical decision rule (see below).

Parameter Condition Points
Current Age 1 – 9 years old (vs. ≥10 years = 0 pts) +12
Hypertriglyceridemia Onset If currently ≥10 years old: onset of HTG at <10 years old +12
Body Mass Index (BMI) < 25.0 kg/m² in adults (or < 85th percentile in children/adolescents) +9
Abdominal Pain / Pancreatitis History of acute pancreatitis (with or without abdominal pain) +16
Recurrent abdominal pain, no history of pancreatitis +9
Secondary Factors No secondary factors contributing to HTG (vs. ≥1 factor = 0 pts) +11
Laboratory Values All fasting triglyceride measurements > 880 mg/dL (> 10 mmol/L) +13
Triglyceride / Total Cholesterol (TG/TC) ratio > 8 (in mg/dL) +8
Apolipoprotein B (ApoB) < 1.0 g/L (< 100 mg/dL) +12
Interaction Bonus Points Age 1–9 years old AND no secondary factors +7
TG/TC ratio > 8 AND ApoB < 1.0 g/L +7
TG/TC ratio > 8 AND no secondary factors +5
Interpretation ≥ 60 pts: Definite FCS  |  45–59 pts: Likely FCS (genetic testing recommended)  |  30–44 pts: Possible FCS (indeterminate; consider genetic testing)  |  < 30 pts: Unlikely FCS (likely MCS)

Reference 2: Hypertriglyceridemia & Chylomicronemia Therapies (FCS vs. MCS)

Expected TG reduction, efficacy in LPL-deficient FCS vs. Multifactorial Chylomicronemia Syndrome (MCS), and key precautions.

Therapy / Class Dosing & Route Expected TG Drop (MCS) Efficacy in Monogenic FCS Clinical Role & Precautions
Strict Very-Low-Fat Diet + MCT Oil Dietary fat <10–15% of calories (15–20 g/day), zero alcohol, MCT oil −50% to −80% Essential Foundation Cornerstone of FCS care (halts intestinal chylomicron assembly). MCTs bypass lymphatics via portal vein. Monitor vitamins A, D, E, K and essential fatty acids.
Olezarsen (Tryngolza)
APOC3 antisense oligonucleotide		 80 mg SubQ once monthly −50% to −60% −43% to −53%
(~88% ↓ pancreatitis)		 FDA-approved for FCS (Balance trial); clears TG via LPL-independent hepatic uptake. Monitor platelets and liver enzymes; injection-site reactions.
Plozasiran
APOC3 siRNA		 25 mg SubQ every 3 months −60% to −75% ~−80%
(PALISADE trial)		 Silences hepatic APOC3 mRNA; marked TG and pancreatitis reduction in genetically confirmed and clinical FCS. Monitor mild glycemic changes.
Fibrates
Fenofibrate, gemfibrozil		 Fenofibrate 145–160 mg PO daily (adjust for eGFR) −30% to −50% Minimal / None
(requires functional LPL)		 1st-line pharmacotherapy for MCS with TG ≥500 mg/dL to prevent pancreatitis. Never combine gemfibrozil with statins (rhabdomyolysis); monitor creatinine & gallstones.
Prescription Omega-3 Fatty Acids
Icosapent ethyl (Vascepa); omega-3-acid ethyl esters		 2 g PO twice daily with meals (4 g/day total) −20% to −30% Minimal / None Icosapent ethyl reduced MACE by 25% in REDUCE-IT (statin-treated ASCVD/diabetes with TG 150–499 mg/dL). Slight increase in atrial fibrillation and bleeding risk.

Acute Hypertriglyceridemic Pancreatitis Protocol NPO, IV fluids, IV regular insulin 0.1–0.3 U/kg/hr (+ D5/D10), ± plasmapheresis −50% to −70% in 24–48h NPO halts chylomicrons;
insulin ineffective if zero LPL		 Used during acute pancreatitis with TG >1,000 mg/dL until TG <500 mg/dL. Note: IV insulin stimulates LPL and works rapidly in MCS/DKA, whereas NPO fasting drives clearance in complete LPL deficiency.
