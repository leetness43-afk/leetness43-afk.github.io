Special Dosing Considerations & Modifications:

  • Acute Illness / Diet / Exercise: Adjustment of insulin icodec dose is not advised during acute illness or short-term changes in physical activity/diet due to its long half-life. Consider adjusting glucose intake or concomitant glucose-lowering therapy instead.
  • Renal or Hepatic Impairment: No specific dosage adjustment is necessary. However, intensify blood glucose monitoring and adjust dosing on an individual basis as required for all insulin therapies.
  • Concomitant Therapy: Closely monitor blood glucose. Timing and doses of concurrent short-acting/rapid-acting insulins or other antidiabetic agents may require adjustment.
