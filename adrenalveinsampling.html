Adrenal Vein Sampling Interpreter

Check cannulation, lateralization, and contralateral suppression, then copy the table into a note.

1. Protocol and Cutoffs

Stimulated: 5. Unstimulated: 1.4–3 in use (default 2).

LI at or above this value = unilateral.

LI below this value = bilateral. In between = indeterminate.

2. Sample Values

Leave unused samples blank. Values such as <55 are accepted. Aldosterone and cortisol units must be the same across all samples.

AVS Interpretation

SI = AV cortisol / IVC cortisol  |  LI = (aldo/cort dominant) / (aldo/cort nondominant)  |  CSI = (aldo/cort nondominant) / (aldo/cort IVC)

Indices are calculated from unrounded values, so a hand calculation from the rounded ratios can differ slightly in the last digit.

Basis for cutoffs: Selectivity, lateralization, and contralateral suppression cutoffs follow the Endocrine Society primary aldosteronism guideline (Adler et al., 2025), with the LI 3–4 indeterminate zone as also described by Reincke et al. (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology, 2021). The unstimulated default of 2 follows the Australian and New Zealand working group recommendations (Yang et al., Clinical Endocrinology, 2025). Epinephrine thresholds are from a single-center cosyntropin-stimulated cohort (Dream et al., Endocrine Practice, 2022).
