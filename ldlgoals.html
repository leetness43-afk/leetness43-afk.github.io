How the estimate is made

Percent reductions are multiplied, not added: Predicted LDL = Current × (1 − %Drop₁) × (1 − %Drop₂) …

Estimated untreated LDL-C = current LDL-C ÷ the fraction remaining on current therapy.

Values used: statin by drug and dose (see box 2); ezetimibe 22% on a statin, 18% alone; bempedoic acid 17% on a statin, 22% alone; PCSK9 antibody 58%; inclisiran 50%; enlicitide 50%; colesevelam 15%.

This is an approximation that has not been formally validated, and individual response varies widely. Confirm every step with a lipid panel at 4–12 weeks.

Clinical Pearl: Each doubling of a statin dose adds only about 4–6% more LDL-C reduction (“Rule of 6”), whereas adding ezetimibe 10 mg to a statin adds about 21–27%. Coverage steps are typical patterns only; payer rules vary.

Evidence Notes

Statin dosing in Asian patients
  • Rosuvastatin: about 2-fold higher exposure in Asian patients; FDA labeling says start 5 mg once daily and weigh risk and benefit if not controlled at doses up to 20 mg. This is not an absolute 20 mg maximum.
  • 2026 ACC/AHA: start with lower doses, especially in East Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) ancestry.
  • RACING (Korea): rosuvastatin 10 mg + ezetimibe 10 mg was non-inferior to rosuvastatin 20 mg for 3-year cardiovascular outcomes, with more patients at LDL-C under 70 mg/dL and less intolerance.
  • Simvastatin: do not combine with lipid-modifying niacin in Chinese patients (myopathy in HPS2-THRIVE).
  • No FDA Asian-specific starting dose exists for atorvastatin, pitavastatin or pravastatin. South Asian ancestry is an ASCVD risk enhancer; do not undertreat.
Goals and monitoring
  • Judge response on both the absolute goal and the percent reduction (at least 50% for goals of 70 or lower).
  • Secondary targets once LDL-C is at goal: non-HDL-C under 130 / 100 / 85; apoB under 90 / 70 / 55 mg/dL (ACC/AHA 2026) for LDL-C goals of 100 / 70 / 55. apoB is preferred when triglycerides are 200 mg/dL or higher.
  • Measure Lp(a) once in all adults; 125 nmol/L (50 mg/dL) or higher is elevated.
  • Lipid panel 4–12 weeks after each change, then every 6–12 months.
  • LDL-C 190 mg/dL or higher: exclude secondary causes, evaluate for familial hypercholesterolemia, cascade-screen relatives.
Statin intolerance
  • NLA 2022: complete intolerance requires failure of at least 2 statins, one at the lowest approved daily dose.
  • In blinded trials the statin-attributable excess of muscle symptoms is small (CTT: relative risk 1.03). In SAMSON, about 90% of the symptom burden on statin was also present on placebo, and half of participants restarted a statin.
  • Rechallenge (different statin, lower dose, or non-daily dosing) before labelling a patient completely intolerant.
Pregnancy and model limits
  • Pregnancy: stop statins 1–2 months before conception or when pregnancy is recognized unless very high risk (for example homozygous FH or established ASCVD). Bile acid sequestrants are the usual option. Avoid ezetimibe, bempedoic acid and PCSK9-targeted agents.
  • PCSK9 antibodies give a similar relative reduction on any statin background; bempedoic acid is weaker on a statin and has not been studied on full-dose high-intensity statins.
  • About 1 in 5 patients on a high-intensity statin achieve less than a 30% reduction; predicted values are averages.
